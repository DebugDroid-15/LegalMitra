## 2. Historical Legal Versions

Statutory chunks are classified across distinct historical operational regimes:

- **Pre-1976 Constitutional Regime**: Original Article 368 formulation prior to 42nd Amendment changes; pre-44th Amendment property rights under Article 19(1)(f) and Article 31.
- **Pre-2018 Specific Relief Regime**: Discretionary specific performance under unamended SRA 1963 Section 10 versus mandatory specific performance with limited exceptional defenses post-2018 Amendment.
- **Pre-2018 Commercial Courts Regime**: Specified value threshold of Rs. 1 Crore versus current reduced threshold of Rs. 3 Lakhs; optional settlement versus mandatory Section 12A pre-institution mediation.
- **Pre-2019 Consumer Protection Regime**: Three-tier system under CPA 1986 (District Forum, State Commission, National Commission) with Rs. 20 Lakh / Rs. 1 Crore thresholds versus CPA 2019 revised pecuniary thresholds, mediation cells, and e-commerce liability.
- **Pre-2024 Criminal Law Regime**: Tripartite penal and procedural architecture (IPC 1860, CrPC 1973, IEA 1872) versus contemporary criminal codes (BNS 2023, BNSS 2023, BSA 2023).

## 3. General Clauses Act, 1897 Application

LegalMitra integrates Section 6 of the General Clauses Act, 1897 (`IND-STAT-GCA-1897`), which provides statutory protection upon repeal:
- Unless a different intention appears, the repeal of an enactment shall not:
  - Revive anything not in force or existing at the time when the repeal takes effect.
  - Affect the previous operation of any enactment so repealed or anything duly done or suffered thereunder.
  - Affect any right, privilege, obligation or liability acquired, accrued or incurred under any enactment so repealed.
  - Affect any penalty, forfeiture or punishment incurred in respect of any offence committed against any enactment so repealed.
  - Affect any investigation, legal proceeding or remedy in respect of any such right, privilege, obligation, liability, penalty, forfeiture or punishment.

This principle is directly encoded in LegalMitra's temporal evaluation engine to prevent legal advice hallucinations regarding repealed laws.
