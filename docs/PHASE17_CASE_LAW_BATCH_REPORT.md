## 1. Prioritization Rationale & Scope

Rather than scraping arbitrary thousands of routine district bail orders, Phase 17 prioritized high-impact decisions answering the core unresolved statutory dilemmas facing Indian legal research:

1. **Electronic Evidence Under New Criminal Laws**:
   - The transition from Indian Evidence Act Section 65B to **Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 Section 63** requires exact alignment with *Arjun Panditrao Khotkar* (2020) and *Anvar P.V.* (2014) to prevent trial judges from mistakenly relying on overruled precedents like *Shafhi Mohammad*.
2. **Arrest & Pre-Trial Liberty Under BNSS**:
   - Incarceration before conviction under BNSS Sections 35, 479, and 482 is governed by the national guidelines in *Satender Kumar Antil v. CBI* (2022).
3. **Arbitration Tribunal Autonomy**:
   - Section 12(5) and the Seventh Schedule disqualification of interested sole arbitrator appointments under *Perkins Eastman Architects DPC* (2020).
4. **Contractual Frustration in Commercial Law**:
   - Doctrine of frustration under Indian Contract Act Section 56 governed by *Satyabrata Ghose* (1954).
5. **Urban Tenancy Disputes in NCT of Delhi**:
   - Severance of the residential restriction under Section 14(1)(e) Delhi Rent Control Act by *Satyawati Sharma* (2008).
6. **Informational Privacy & Fundamental Rights**:
   - Constitutional architecture of privacy under Article 21 and Part III governed by *K.S. Puttaswamy* (2017).
