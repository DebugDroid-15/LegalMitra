## 2. Critical Jurisprudential Rule: Enactment Does Not Equal Commencement

Under Section 1(3) of each of the four Labour Codes:
> *"It shall come into force on such date as the Central Government may, by notification in the Official Gazette, appoint; and different dates may be appointed for different provisions of this Code..."*

Because the Central Government has not issued general commencement notifications under Section 1(3), and state governments have not finalized synchronized rules across all 28 states and 8 union territories, **the predecessor Acts remain the sole governing substantive law of India**.

Any legal tool advising that the Industrial Disputes Act 1947 or Factories Act 1948 have been repealed as of 2024–2026 commits a catastrophic malpractice error. LegalMitra enforces this distinction programmatically.
