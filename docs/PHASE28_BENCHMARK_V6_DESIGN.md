## 1. Executive Summary & Design Principles

Benchmark v6 (`data/17_evaluation/evaluation_cases_v6.json`) expands LegalMitra's evaluation corpus to **68 total cases**. It preserves all previous versions (v1 through v5) unmodified in their respective versioned directories (`data/12_evaluation/` through `data/16_evaluation/`).

Benchmark v6 was designed to avoid evaluation shortcuts, ensure test leakage prevention, and introduce independent legal answer validation across claim-level grounding, temporal dates, and jurisdictional barriers.

## 4. Evaluation Leakage Prevention Verification

To eliminate evaluation leakage:
1. **Production Engine Isolation:** Production responses generated by `LegalRAGEngine.answer()` do NOT read or parse benchmark cases, expected answers, golden citations, or rationales.
2. **Dynamic Claim Audit:** Claims are extracted dynamically from generated response paragraphs and verified against retrieved chunk contents, not against pre-baked strings.
3. **Strict Fallback Rules:** Empty results or timeouts trigger explicit failure reasons unless the benchmark case explicitly mandates `should_abstain = True`.
4. **Mock Provider Separation:** `MockLLMProvider` is equipped with native extractive synthesis mimicking production LLM formatting without hard-coded case IDs.
