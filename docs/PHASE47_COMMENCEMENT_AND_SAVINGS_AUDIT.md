4. **Section 143 of OSH Code, 2020**

---

## 2. Savings Mechanics under Section 104 IR Code 2020

Even when Section 104 is commenced in the future, its savings sub-sections preserve historical actions:
- **Section 104(2)(a)**: Previous operations of repealed enactments preserved.
- **Section 104(2)(c)**: Any right, privilege, obligation, or liability acquired, accrued, or incurred under repealed enactments preserved.
- **Section 104(2)(e)**: Any investigation, legal proceeding, or remedy in respect of any such right, penalty, forfeiture, or punishment continues as if the Code had not been passed.

## 3. Retrenchment Invariant: Industrial Disputes Act 1947 Section 25F

In any labor dispute arising today (e.g. October 2024 or 2026):
1. **Notice or Payment**: 1 month's prior notice in writing stating reasons, or wages in lieu (s.25F(a)).
2. **Statutory Compensation**: 15 days average pay for every completed year of continuous service or part in excess of 6 months (s.25F(b)).
3. **Government Notice**: Notice in prescribed form to the appropriate Government (s.25F(c)).
4. **Continuous Service Calculation**: Under *Workmen of American Express v. Management* (1985) 4 SCC 71, continuous service of 240 days under Section 25B includes Sundays, weekly offs, and paid holidays.
