## 1. Statutory Context & Federal Architecture

Article 246 and the Seventh Schedule (List II - State List, List III - Concurrent List) of the Constitution of India establish the legislative powers of State Legislatures and Union Territories with legislative assemblies (NCT of Delhi, Puducherry, Jammu & Kashmir).

To build a genuinely national Indian legal intelligence platform, LegalMitra maintains an exhaustive **36-Jurisdiction Registry** (`STATE_UT_REGISTRY`) spanning all 28 States and 8 Union Territories.

Data files produced:
