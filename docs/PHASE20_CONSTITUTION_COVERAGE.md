## 3. Constitutional Amendment Acts Registered

LegalMitra tracks foundational Constitutional Amendment Acts within its statutory and temporal knowledge model:

1. **Constitution (First Amendment) Act, 1951**: Validated zamindari abolition, inserted Article 31A/31B and the Ninth Schedule, amended Article 19(2).
2. **Constitution (Twenty-Fourth Amendment) Act, 1971**: Affirmed parliamentary power to amend fundamental rights under Article 368 following *Golak Nath*.
3. **Constitution (Forty-Second Amendment) Act, 1976**: Substantive restructuring, inserted "Socialist, Secular, Integrity" in Preamble, Part IVA (Fundamental Duties), and Part XIVA (Tribunals).
4. **Constitution (Forty-Fourth Amendment) Act, 1978**: Restored fundamental freedoms post-emergency, omitted Article 19(1)(f) and Article 31, inserted Article 300A.
5. **Constitution (Eighty-Sixth Amendment) Act, 2002**: Inserted Article 21A making elementary education a fundamental right.
6. **Constitution (One Hundred and Sixth Amendment) Act, 2023**: *Nari Shakti Vandan Adhiniyam*, providing one-third reservation for women in Lok Sabha and State Legislative Assemblies.
