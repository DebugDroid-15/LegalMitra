# LegalMitra Phase 34: Jurisdiction & Legal Domain Coverage Report

**Status:** `VERIFIED`  
**Execution Date:** 2026-10-18  

---

## 1. Executive Summary

Phase 34 broadened LegalMitra's geographic and institutional coverage across multiple Indian High Courts and State jurisdictions. The pretraining corpus now contains direct legislative and precedent representations from 4 distinct States/UTs and 4 major High Courts, in addition to the Supreme Court of India and Central Union legislation.

### Identified Coverage Gaps for Future Phases
1. **High Court Precedent Depth:** Allahabad High Court (the largest High Court in India by volume) and Punjab & Haryana High Court are currently unrepresented.
2. **Specialized Tribunals:** National Company Law Appellate Tribunal (NCLAT), National Green Tribunal (NGT), and Telecom Disputes Settlement and Appellate Tribunal (TDSAT) decisions are pending acquisition.
3. **Regional Indian Languages:** Tamil, Bengali, Marathi, and Kannada official statutory gazettes represent prime expansion targets once bilingual English–Hindi pretraining reaches scale.
