JUDGMENT

1. This application under Section 482 of the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 (BNSS) seeks the grant of anticipatory bail in FIR No. 204/2024 registered at Police Station Connaught Place.

2. Learned counsel for the respondent submits that the alleged offences pertain to the newly enacted Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS). It is submitted that the respondent has joined the investigation and co-operated with the investigating officer.

3. The Court has considered the submissions and the case diary. The statutory considerations under Section 482 of BNSS mandate that the Court assess the nature and gravity of the accusation, the antecedents of the applicant, and the possibility of the applicant fleeing from justice.

4. In the facts and circumstances of the present case, custodial interrogation of the respondent is not warranted. In the event of arrest, the applicant shall be admitted to bail subject to furnishing a personal bond of Rs. 25,000/- with one surety of the like amount.

RATIO DECIDENDI: The principles governing the exercise of jurisdiction to grant anticipatory bail under Section 482 of BNSS continue the established constitutional jurisprudence under erstwhile Section 438 of CrPC, requiring balance between personal liberty under Article 21 and investigatory interests of the State.
