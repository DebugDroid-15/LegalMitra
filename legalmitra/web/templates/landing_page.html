Can a High Court quash an FIR in a commercial dispute under Section 528 BNSS (erstwhile Sec 482 CrPC)?
LM
Statutory Short Answer
Jul 2024 • In Force

Yes. Under Section 528 BNSS, 2023 (saving inherent powers formerly under Sec 482 CrPC), High Courts retain jurisdiction to quash criminal proceedings when the dispute is primarily civil or commercial, preventing the process from becoming an engine of harassment.

ACT ANALYSIS Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023 (BNS)
Commenced: 1 July 2024
SECTION 316 BNS • ERSTWHILE SEC 405 IPC

Criminal Breach of Trust

Plain-Language Meaning

Criminal Breach of Trust occurs when an individual is entrusted with property or dominion over property, and subsequently dishonestly misappropriates, converts to their own use, or disposes of that property in violation of law or legal contract.

Essential Ingredients
  • Actual entrustment of property or control
  • Dishonest intention at the moment of conversion
  • Violation of lawful direction or express trust
Doctrinal Distinction

Distinct from Cheating (Sec 318): In Breach of Trust, entrustment is innocent at inception; dishonest mens rea develops subsequently.

Frequently Asked Questions

Understand how LegalMitra indexes Indian statutes, maintains primary sources, and formats research.

What is LegalMitra? ↓

LegalMitra is an AI-powered legal intelligence platform specifically engineered around Indian law. It assists citizens, legal researchers, corporate teams, and advocates by synthesizing complex statutes, court judgments, and procedural guidelines into structured, source-linked explanations.

Which country's laws does LegalMitra focus on? ↓

LegalMitra is exclusively focused on the legal framework of the Republic of India — including Central Acts, State amendments, Supreme Court and High Court precedents, and official Ministry gazette notifications.

Can I ask questions in everyday language? ↓

Yes. You do not need to memorize section numbers or Latin legal terms. You can describe your situation in plain English, and LegalMitra maps your facts to relevant statutory provisions and case precedents.

Does LegalMitra provide links to primary legal sources? ↓

Yes. Every substantive legal response includes a dedicated source panel displaying the governing Act, section number, official Gazette reference, or Supreme Court citation, with links to inspect primary texts whenever indexed.

Can I rely on LegalMitra as legal advice? ↓

No. LegalMitra provides legal information and research assistance, not advocate-client representation or formal legal advice. If you have an active litigation matter or contentious dispute, you should consult an enrolled advocate.

Does the commencement date of an Indian law matter? ↓

Critically so. In India, Parliament often passes an Act that comes into force only when appointed by subsequent Ministry Gazette notifications. LegalMitra tracks these commencement dates so users don't mistakenly cite provisions that are not yet active.
